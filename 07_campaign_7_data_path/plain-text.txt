DATA PATHS BEFORE DEVICES

Make reach, power, latency and ownership clear before approving gateways or sensors.

Most retrofit failures start after procurement. The device works, but the reading cannot reach the system, owner or support team that must act on it.

FAST COMPARISON

M-Bus: Meter networks where many utility readings need stable collection.
Modbus: Industrial equipment values and deterministic local integration.
BACnet: Building-system objects, alarms and supervisory control.
LoRaWAN: Long-range, low-power sensing where new cabling is difficult.

ARCHITECTURE MOVE
Decide the destination system, support owner and failure mode before approving the device schedule.
