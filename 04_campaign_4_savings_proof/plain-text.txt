SAVINGS NEED PROOF, NOT SLIDES

Dubai's next efficiency phase will reward operators who can trace every saving back to a reliable reading.

Regional signal:
Dubai's DSM Strategy 2050 targets at least 30% savings by 2030 and 50% by 2050 compared to business as usual in electricity, water, and transport fuel consumption. The strategy includes 12 programmes and 7 implementation mechanisms.

The useful question:
Can the saving survive finance, operations, and audit?

Proof chain:
Baseline -> Live reads -> Exceptions -> M&V -> Decision

Boardroom move:
Ask every efficiency proposal for its proof chain before approving its payback.
