THE FIRST LEAK SIGNAL SHOULD NOT BE THE WATER BILL

DEWA's leak-alert data shows why anomaly-to-action time belongs on the operations dashboard.

DEWA reported that its High Water Usage Alert service identified 2,663,444 post-meter leaks over six years to 31 December 2024. The service contributed to a reported reduction of 266,706 tonnes of carbon emissions over the same period.

THE USEFUL OPERATING LOOP

1. Baseline: Learn normal use by hour, asset and zone.
2. Anomaly: Flag the unusual pattern while it is small.
3. Action: Route, repair and confirm the return to normal.

PORTFOLIO MOVE
Track anomaly-to-work-order and work-order-to-resolution time across every site.
