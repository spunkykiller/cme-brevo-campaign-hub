CHOOSE THE DATA PATH BEFORE THE DEVICE

Reach, power, latency and ownership should decide the architecture before the device count grows.

Most retrofit problems begin after procurement: the sensor works, but its data cannot travel reliably into the system that must use it.

FAST COMPARISON

M-Bus: Purpose-built meter collection across dense utility networks.
Modbus: Industrial equipment data and deterministic local integration.
BACnet: Building-system objects, alarms and supervisory control.
LoRaWAN: Long-range, low-power sensing where new cabling is difficult.

ARCHITECTURE MOVE
Define the destination system, support owner and failure mode before approving the device schedule.
