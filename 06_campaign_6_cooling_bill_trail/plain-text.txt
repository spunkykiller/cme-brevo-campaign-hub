WHEN COOLING COSTS RISE, FOLLOW THE METERING TRAIL

Dubai's district-cooling framework makes the meter, allocation and invoice one continuous evidence chain.

Fair cooling charges depend on more than a working BTU meter. Four confidence checks matter:

1. Meter health: reading continuity, calibration and fault history.
2. Thermal inputs: flow and temperature values make energy visible.
3. Tenant allocation: the right consumption reaches the right account.
4. Billing logic: tariffs and adjustments remain reviewable.

Meter -> Validate -> Allocate -> Invoice
